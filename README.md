# DiceCatcher

Base game inspired by the tutorial, with simple assets and behavior for now.

## Future Direction

The goal is to turn it into a game with its own identity, more anime/chibi, more frantic, and with progression between runs.

## Visual Direction

- Replace the sprites with original art in an anime chibi style.
- Replace the fox-like main character with an original animal/furry girl design.
- Make the Dice look like cakes, sweets, or other more eye-catching skins.
- Add background animations when combos get high or when the music intensifies.

## Planned Gameplay

- Good Dice will keep falling, and they should not be allowed to drop.
- At the start, only normal Dice will appear.
- As progress and money are earned, Dice with different powers and behaviors can be unlocked.
- Some Dice will grant positive effects such as speed or invulnerability.
- Others will be dangerous and apply negative effects such as slowing or freezing, so they should not be caught.
- A life system should be added so the punishment is not just an instant loss.

## Economy and Progression

- Each caught Dice will give money.
- Sometimes coins or money balls will also fall as extra rewards.
- Money will be used to unlock new Dice types, better rewards, and more lives.
- Gadgets or assists with limited uses per run could also be purchased, for example up to two times.

## Difficulty

- Difficulty will increase over time.
- The speed, frequency, and variety of the Dice will grow as the run progresses.
- Music and visual effects will support that rise in tension.

## Audio

- Replace or complement the music with more energetic tracks.
- If needed, use AI-generated music for prototypes or early versions.

## Long-Term Goal

The idea is for each run to feel more rewarding, more controlled-chaotic, and more visually distinctive, with a presentation that feels more alive and closer to an anime/chibi arcade game.
